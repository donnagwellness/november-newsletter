Nourished · In my kitchen

Tammy's Christmas Mango Salad

Serves 6 as a side10 minutesNo cooking

This one is close to my heart. A dear friend made this salad at every Christmas gathering, and it always disappeared first. I'm sharing it this month in her memory, so it can find its way onto your table too.

You'll need

  • 3 ripe mangoes, diced
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • A good handful of fresh mint leaves, torn

Dressing

  • 1 long red chilli, finely chopped (remove the seeds for a milder kick)
  • Juice of 1–2 limes
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Whisk the chilli, lime juice, sesame oil and salt together in a small bowl.
  2. Place the diced mango and spring onion in a serving bowl.
  3. Pour over the dressing and toss gently so the mango keeps its shape.
  4. Taste and adjust, adding more lime, chilli or salt until it's just right for you.
  5. Scatter the mint over just before serving.

Tip: it's lovely alongside barbecued prawns, chicken or fish, and the quantities are just a guide.
